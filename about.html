About Pintare Equipamentos

Painting the Future of Industry, Today.

Pintare Equipamentos Para Pintura LTDA. has been a leading provider of industrial painting equipment in Brazil for over two decades. Founded in 2002, our company has grown from a small local supplier to a nationwide distributor, serving a diverse range of industries, including automotive, aerospace, construction, and manufacturing.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower businesses with high-quality, reliable painting equipment that enhances efficiency, reduces costs, and delivers superior results. We are committed to providing exceptional customer service, expert technical support, and timely nationwide delivery.

Our Commitment

We are dedicated to building long-term partnerships with our clients, understanding their unique needs, and providing tailored solutions. Our commitment to excellence extends beyond our products to encompass every aspect of our customer interactions.
